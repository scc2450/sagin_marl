\begin{abstract}
Space-air-ground integrated networks require dynamic scheduling policies that
can coordinate mobile unmanned aerial vehicles, intermittent satellite
visibility, shared bandwidth, and stochastic service demand. This paper studies
a topology-aware multi-agent scheduling framework for UAV-satellite integrated
networks under queue, mobility, visibility, and safety constraints. The method
uses a structured learning scheduler with staged decisions for UAV movement,
satellite association, and access bandwidth allocation. We evaluate the learned
policy against queue-aware, Lyapunov/MaxWeight, and topology-aware heuristic
baselines across source, perturbed, and nearby scale-transfer scenarios. The
current abstract is a placeholder and will be rewritten after the final claim
set and experiment matrix are frozen.
\end{abstract}
